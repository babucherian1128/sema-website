Preserving Our Heritage,

SEMA is dedicated to fostering a vibrant home for the Malayalee diaspora in South Edmonton. We bridge generations through culture, sports, and a spirit of selfless service.

Our Mission

To unite the Malayalee community in South Edmonton by promoting our unique cultural identity, supporting newcomers, and contributing actively to the social fabric of Alberta through heritage-led initiatives.

visibility

Our Vision

A flourishing, inclusive, and socially conscious community where every Malayalee in South Edmonton feels connected to their roots while thriving in their Canadian journey.

The SEMA Journey

2015

Humble Beginnings

Starting with just 10 families, SEMA was founded in a living room in Ellerslie, driven by the desire to keep our language and traditions alive for our children.

fiber_manual_record
2020

Pivoting to Service

During the global pandemic, SEMA transitioned to a support network, providing groceries and emotional support to quarantined families and international students.

fiber_manual_record
2024

A Regional Powerhouse

Now representing over 500 members, SEMA is a registered non-profit and a key partner for regional cultural festivals and sporting events.
