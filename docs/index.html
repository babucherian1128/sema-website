About SEMA

Preserving Heritage, Building Community

South Edmonton Malayalee Association (SEMA) is dedicated to fostering a strong sense of belonging among Malayalees in Edmonton. We strive to keep the essence of God's Own Country alive through cultural exchange and community support.

From the grand celebrations of Onam and Vishu to the festive warmth of Christmas and New Year, we bring families together to celebrate our roots. Our commitment extends beyond festivities to social responsibility, organizing regular blood donation drives and community outreach programs.
