Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 11, 2026

SEMA currently operates this website as a static informational site hosted on GitHub Pages. If you contact us through email links or choose to subscribe to updates, the information you send is used only to respond to your request, coordinate community activities, or share relevant SEMA updates.

We do not currently run an automated member portal or online payment flow on this website. Information submitted through your email app is handled by SEMA administrators and volunteers for community operations only.

Photos shared with SEMA may be used for community communications, event recaps, and cultural promotion unless you ask us not to use them. If you want any photo or personal information removed, email info@semaedmonton.ca.

We may update this policy as the website grows. Material updates will be reflected on this page.
